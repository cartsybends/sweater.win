# Team mini-games

Who Am I? uses the builder's historical regular-season team records, including goalies. Every four-choice question contains alumni of the selected club. Its position/era, games played, and peak season clues are derived from those records rather than hand-written guesses. Classic selects established alumni; Challenge includes the wider eligible pool.

Team Journey uses `team_careers.json`, generated by `tools/team_games/update_careers.py`. The official NHL player landing endpoint supplies NHL regular-season appearances; their sum must reconcile with the complete career games-played total. Every season with multiple clubs is ordered by dated game logs, whose per-team appearance counts must exactly match the season totals. Unverified new careers are excluded; a failed refresh preserves the last verified record. Active careers refresh weekly during the existing site build.

Return visits remain separate stops. Identical club tiles are interchangeable during play, and dates are revealed by the correct career position. Phoenix/Arizona is one club with a combined label; the original Winnipeg Jets, modern Jets, and Utah have distinct identities. Classic uses 3–5 stops; Challenge uses 6–10.

Both games contain five unique players and award up to 50 points. Analytics records anonymous run totals and answer steps under `team_identity` and `team_journey`; player IDs, names, answers and career paths are not transmitted. Historical analytics labels remain available for the retired team modes.
