How Sweater calculates this

Team scoring and goals allowed are blended with previous seasons and a league-average prior. Each simulated season draws plausible team scoring rates, then plays the remaining NHL schedule with home ice, overtime, and shootouts. Tied standings use regulation wins, ROW, total wins, head-to-head results with the odd game excluded, goal differential, and goals scored.

Division places and wild cards form a fixed playoff bracket. Each round is best-of-seven with home ice; confirmed playoff results remain fixed. The model estimates team strength from goals and results; it does not currently model injuries, projected lineups, starting goalies, or expected goals.

These are Sweater model estimates. Tiny probabilities display as <0.1% and >99.9%; simulation extremes do not establish mathematical elimination or clinching. History begins when this model starts recording forecasts. A five-minute scheduled check looks for completed games; checks can be delayed. The browser checks for published forecasts every two minutes.
