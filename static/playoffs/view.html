Data definitions and model methodology

Season records, goals, final scores, and shots come from NHL feeds. Win % is wins divided by games played. Points % is points divided by twice games played. Last 10 uses each club’s ten most recent completed regular-season games.

Team scoring and goals allowed are blended with previous seasons and a league-average prior. Each simulated season draws plausible team scoring rates, then plays the remaining NHL schedule with home ice, overtime, and shootouts. Tiebreakers include RW, ROW, total wins, head-to-head results, goal differential, and goals scored. A fixed best-of-seven playoff bracket determines each stage’s probabilities.

Game win probabilities use the model’s mean scoring rates. Estimates saved before puck drop are frozen when the game begins. Older games marked “Reconstructed” are rebuilt using only final results from earlier calendar dates; they are not saved historical forecasts or betting prices. Shooting percentages exclude the goal awarded for a shootout win.

These are beta model estimates, without injury, lineup, starting-goalie, or shot-based expected-goal inputs. Tiny probabilities display as <0.1% and >99.9%; simulation extremes do not establish mathematical clinching or elimination. A five-minute scheduled check looks for new results; checks can be delayed. The browser checks for published data every two minutes.
