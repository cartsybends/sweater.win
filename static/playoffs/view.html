Data definitions and model methodology

Season records, goals, final scores, and shots come from NHL feeds. Win % is wins divided by games played. Points % is points divided by twice games played. Last 10 uses each club’s ten most recent completed regular-season games.

The earlier goals-only model blends scoring and goals allowed with previous seasons and a league-average prior.

Each simulated season draws plausible team strengths and plays the remaining NHL schedule. Game forecasts and season simulations share the same strength model. Tiebreakers include RW, ROW, total wins, head-to-head results, goal differential, and goals scored. A fixed best-of-seven playoff bracket determines each stage’s probabilities; future playoff goalie assignments and rest are uncertain.

Estimates saved before puck drop are frozen when a game begins, including forecasts saved by a previous model version. Older games marked “Reconstructed” use only earlier calendar dates and do not include today’s injury reports or later starter confirmations. Shooting percentages exclude the goal awarded for a shootout win.

These are beta estimates. Skater injury reports are shown as context; a reliable individual skater injury-impact adjustment is not yet validated. Confirmed goalie reports link to their source; missing or conflicting reports retain a projected goalie distribution. Listed injury return dates are estimates, not confirmed future availability.

Tiny probabilities display as <0.1% and >99.9%; simulation extremes do not establish mathematical clinching or elimination. Full forecast checks target fifteen minutes and can be delayed. On Games, live win percentages use a separate Cloudflare feed checked about every thirty seconds while the tab is visible. Advanced game statistics update on their source’s nightly schedule. The browser checks for published forecasts every two minutes.

Advanced hockey data: MoneyPuck.com. MoneyPuck supplies data inputs, not Sweater’s final predictions. Sportsbook quotes and injury reporting are supplied through ESPN; each available quote identifies its bookmaker and observation time. Starter news is read automatically from RotoWire’s public NHL RSS feed, respecting its ten-minute cache interval. RSS can omit reports or publish after the original announcement; expected or morning-skate reports do not count as confirmations.
