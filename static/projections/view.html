    How these projections work

    Current points per game = (goals + assists) ÷ games played. The main projection starts with recorded points and adds the expected points over the remaining schedule. Your sliders change future scoring and future missed games, never points already recorded.

    Player mode uses the current club’s games played to account for the team’s remaining schedule; Calculator mode uses the games you entered. Totals across traded clubs are combined. Unknown current clubs use the player’s games played as a manual starting assumption. The chart’s full-season pace guide shows the current rate multiplied by the selected season length; it can differ from the main projection when a player has already missed games.

    These are pace estimates, not a prediction model. Early-season samples, role changes and future availability can change the result. Goal and assist estimates are rounded separately. NHL stats are checked about every 15 minutes, independently of the daily games. Open player projections check for a newer snapshot every five minutes and when you return to the tab. NHL season totals can lag live play, and scheduled updates may occasionally be delayed. The checked time shows the last successful NHL collection. Shared links retain the stat line and checked time captured by their sender.
