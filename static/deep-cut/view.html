Rules & scoring

Seven prompts. Three seconds to read, then 35 seconds to name one qualifying player. Wrong or unrecognized names cost three seconds. A pass or expired clock scores zero. The clock keeps running if you leave an active prompt.

Answer corrections are on by default: common nicknames, unique surnames, useful partial names, and close spellings can be matched to a qualifying player. Ambiguous names need more detail. Turn corrections off to require an exact full name. Capitalization, accents and punctuation never matter. Each point moves your skater 10 metres; 700 points is a perfect shift. At least 300 points counts as a daily win.

Current-club players score 10; the six lowest average-TOI current skaters for that club score 15. Current-club goalies stay at 10. Rosters and usage are fixed per shift.

Rarity tiers combine estimates from career games, awards and team appearances with editorial reviews of name recognition and the specific connection. They are not live player-pick percentages. Cult legends are selected for a satisfying obscure connection. Everyone receives the same daily set and scores; practice is unranked. Career, award and country facts are frozen through 2025–26; new team prompts include verified later appearances. Predecessor franchises are separate.
