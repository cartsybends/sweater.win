Rules & scoring

Seven prompts. Three seconds to read, then 35 seconds to name one qualifying player. Wrong or unrecognized names cost three seconds. A pass or expired clock scores zero. The clock keeps running if you leave an active prompt.

Full names, unique surnames, common aliases, accents, and small typos are supported. Ambiguous names need more detail. Each point moves your skater 10 metres; 700 points is a perfect shift. At least 300 points counts as a daily win.

Rarity tiers are fixed editorial estimates from career games, awards, Hall of Fame status, era, and team appearances. They are not live player-pick percentages. Cult legends are selected for a satisfying obscure connection. Everyone receives the same daily set and scores; practice is unranked. Player facts are frozen through 2025–26, and predecessor franchises are separate.
