# Who Scored? reported highlight corrections

All 41 reported highlights keep their clip IDs and credited scorers. The revised
cuts show a recognizable complete scoring play and its finish or aftermath;
a visible puck crossing the line is not required.

Robertson, Zibanejad, Crouse and Coleman use replacement official goals. Coleman’s
previous empty-net highlight has been replaced by his March 14, 2025 goal against
Colorado. The remaining edits use revised scoring windows and repaired masks.

The original/masked frame review covers 3,953 frames, including full-scene scorer
identity, jersey coverage, faces, helmets, gloves, overlaps and camera exits.
Both compressed streams were fully decoded and visually sampled after review.
`review.json` pins each source, edit and exported asset to its checksum; the
correction gate retains all 79 scorer IDs and the unaffected library entries.

The setup screen displays the actual active footage range, **2019–2025**, with a
note that players may have changed teams. Local validation and live publication
are recorded separately in `verification.json` and `live-verification.json`.
